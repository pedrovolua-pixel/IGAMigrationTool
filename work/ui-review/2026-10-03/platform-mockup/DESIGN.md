# Complete platform mockup

Visual thesis: extend the recovered light, chart-led assessment workspace with its blue accent, calm spacing, and unblurred contextual overlays.

Content plan: preserve the original overview as the entry point; expose operational areas in grouped navigation; use charts, evidence-linked lists, and inspectors for detail; keep one primary action per page. Include AI and settings in depth, plus explicitly labeled previews of later phases.

Interaction thesis: brief page entrance, clear navigation hover/selection, and a restrained overlay slide preserve orientation. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

This is a separate, fictional-data prototype for review. It does not modify application source, call APIs, run AI, connect to databases, publish reports, or execute migration. Local controls simulate the proposed flow. AI answers are predefined and labeled. Settings persist only in this browser. The recovered prototype remains available separately on port 5210.
