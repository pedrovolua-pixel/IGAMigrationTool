## Scope and evidence

This is the executable acceptance design for [EV02-R01–03](local-evaluation-reviewer-contract-proposal.md), tracing FR-HAS-11/13/15/50–53, AC-HAS-12/19 and bounded TP-HAS-012/019. It does not replace the full feature [test plan](test-plan.md), [evaluation plan](evaluation-plan.md), [authorization matrix](../../docs/security/health-assessment-authorization-matrix.md) or [audit policy](../../docs/security/health-assessment-audit-policy.md).

Prepare independent expected outcomes before implementation. A versioned synthetic fixture registry supplies identities/qualifications, role/action/category assignments, scope, validity/revocation, conflict disclosures and reasoned clearances. Review inputs cannot modify that trusted registry. Fixture identifiers, source locks and customer approval declarations are explicitly synthetic; no real customer payload, credential, raw source resolver or provider is used. Do not reuse the implementation's policy decision or arithmetic method as the expected-value oracle.

Each future result records stable test ID, exact source/fixture/registry/policy/instruction versions and digests, tool invocation, expected/actual result, limitations and attributable nonauthor review. Proposal source/link/whitespace/secret checks are documentary evidence only. Unit/contract/property, later integration/UI/API/queued-work, actual identity and two-environment pilot results are distinct; no documentary PASS is a runtime PASS.
