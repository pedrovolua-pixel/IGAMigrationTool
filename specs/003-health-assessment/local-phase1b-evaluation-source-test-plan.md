For the existing normal fixed Phase1B run: twelve coverage keys, ten deterministic plus two AI; one AI work; two saved AI Proposed80/weight1 originals; schedule/High and retry/Medium, OPERATIONS/SyntheticOperations; two native root-cause groups, one original occurrence/object each. Evidence references remain exactly ev-111…111 and ev-222…222, bound to their respective original proposal/packet/attempt. There is one fixed source environment. Capture must not produce100 members, a sampler manifest, a reviewer vote, a confidence-band alias or a second environment. No evaluation accuracy is calculated.

A source with terminal AI gap(s) captures only actual saved generated findings as members and every remaining exact AI terminal outcome as a gap. Two legitimate gaps produce zero members/two gaps, not two unavailable fictional findings. An unchanged successful original remains in the capture after assessment Confirm/Reject/Defer/title/comment events. Original bytes/digests/IDs stay equal to saved AiFindingOriginal; those collaboration records are not evaluation votes.
