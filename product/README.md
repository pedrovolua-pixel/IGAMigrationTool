# Product documentation

This directory is the source of truth for product-wide intent and requirements.

## Discovery baseline

- [`vision.md`](./vision.md) — problem, proposition, outcomes, boundaries, and risks
- [`principles.md`](./principles.md) — proposed product decision principles
- [`personas.md`](./personas.md) — user and stakeholder hypotheses
- [`journeys.md`](./journeys.md) — end-to-end migration and assessment journeys
- [`requirements.md`](./requirements.md) — product-wide functional requirements
- [`non-functional-requirements.md`](./non-functional-requirements.md) — quality, security, reliability, and operational requirements
- [`feature-map.md`](./feature-map.md) — capability map organized by product phase
- [`roadmap.md`](./roadmap.md) — outcome-based sequencing
- [`open-questions.md`](./open-questions.md) — unresolved product decisions and validation needs

The product owner approved the discovery baseline on 2026-09-26 and the reconciled health-pilot decisions on 2026-09-28 for feature specification and architecture work. [`open-questions.md`](./open-questions.md) remains authoritative for explicitly deferred decisions. Approval does not authorize implementation, production operations, or silent resolution of open questions. Technical implementation choices belong in later architecture and technical-design work.
